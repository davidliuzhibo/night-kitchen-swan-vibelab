Night kitchen cookware. Kenney assets are not included.
Self-baked photo-decal cookware (the previous wok, bowl, plate, egg, rice mound,
scallion, and spatula) has been removed.

wok.glb, rice-bowl.glb, egg.glb, scallion.glb, spatula.glb, plate.glb
  Generated with Volcengine doubao-seed3d-2-0, then simplified and resized.
  The delivered files used KHR_draco_mesh_compression as a required extension.
  Spatial SDK 6.1.9 documents glb/gltf and does not ship a Draco decoder, so
  these copies were decoded to standard glTF before packing. A later pass
  reduced them to about 10k triangles and 512 PNG maps so cold start does not
  decode the 30k-face files on the frame loop. See SIZES.md.
